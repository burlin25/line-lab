A MORE USEFUL RESEARCH WORKFLOW

Start with the quote. Then test the hypothesis.

01 / Compare the same market

Read spread and price together. A price at −3.5 cannot be directly compared to a price at −4.5. Check event timing and quote age.

02 / Distinguish disagreement from edge

Book-to-book differences describe the market. Expected value needs an independently tested probability estimate and the offered payout.

03 / Evaluate with settled evidence

Closing-line comparisons, calibration and realized returns need stored snapshots and settled outcomes. Those measures are unavailable here yet.
